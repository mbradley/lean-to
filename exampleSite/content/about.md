This is the demo site for the **Lean-to** theme, a lean two-column Hugo theme for
Micro.blog derived from [Bothy](https://github.com/matthewl/bothy).

This page is a plain Hugo page (no `type: post`), so it renders through
`_default/single.html` rather than the post template — and it appears in the top
navigation because its front matter sets `menu: main`.

The posts on the home page are fixtures that exercise the theme's templates: titled
and untitled posts, a photo post, and a clippings post.
