Titled posts become articles with an `<h2>` headline on the home page and on their own
page. This fixture exercises the long-form path: multiple paragraphs, a subheading, a
blockquote, and a list.

## A subheading

Lean-to keeps the reading column narrow and the typography quiet. Long-form posts and
short notes share the same `e-content` styling, so prose like this should feel
comfortable to read.

> A blockquote, to check indentation, color, and spacing against the body text.

A short list, to check list styling:

- First item
- Second item, slightly longer so it has a chance to wrap and show line height
- Third item

And a closing paragraph with an [inline link](https://example.com/) to confirm link
color in both light and dark themes.
